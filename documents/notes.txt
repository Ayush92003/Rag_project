8. Token

A token is the smallest unit processed by an LLM.

Sentence

I love ChatGPT

may become

"I"

" love"

" Chat"

"GPT"

Not always equal to words.

Approximation

100 words ≈ 130 tokens

12. Top-K Sampling

Choose next word from top K probable words.

Example

Top 5 words

↓

Choose one

K controls diversity.

13. Top-P Sampling

Choose smallest group whose cumulative probability ≥ P.

Usually better than Top-K.

22. Chain of Thought (CoT)

Breaks reasoning into intermediate steps before producing an answer.

23. Embeddings

Embeddings convert text into vectors (lists of numbers) that capture semantic meaning.

Example

"Cat"

↓

[0.31, -0.44, 0.91, ...]

Similar meanings have similar vectors.

25. Semantic Search
